# Vendors and sourcing: Workstream C (Proddatur, target launch 10 Jan 2027)

Prepared 2026-10-13. Labels used throughout:
- **[V]** verified by a web source (listed in Sources)
- **[U]** unverified: general industry knowledge or assumption; confirm locally before relying on it
- **[LOCAL]** must be learned by visiting/phoning in Proddatur/Kadapa; web search found nothing

No vendor names, phone numbers or prices are given. None were found or verified. Numeric price ranges are deliberately omitted; collect real quotes via the RFQ below.

Search findings were thin: web search returned no current Andhra Pradesh broiler rate, no Proddatur slaughterhouse or livestock-market page, and no current Nellore fish/prawn price. Most of this document is therefore a framework plus a discovery plan, not market data.

## 1. Sourcing map per product

### 1.1 Broiler chicken
- **How supply works.** Contract broiler farming is common: an integrator supplies chicks, feed and medicines, farmers grow the birds and get paid growing charges per kg live weight, and the integrator procures the output [V: MEAS/Krishikosh studies]. Named integrators in India include Venkateshwara (Venky's), Suguna, Godrej, Shanti, Jaffa, Skylark [V, same source]. Whether any sells live birds to a new retailer in Proddatur, and on what terms, is [LOCAL].
- **Other channels [U]:** independent (non-contract) farms; local wholesale poultry traders/commission agents who buy from farms and sell live or dressed birds to retailers; retail-shop-level dressed supply.
- **Daily rate setting [U/LOCAL].** Broiler live rates are usually quoted daily by regional trader/farmer associations and WhatsApp rate groups, then retail is set on top. No official source was found. Ask each trader which rate board they follow and agree a written basis (e.g. "live rate of the day at [named market] minus/plus X per kg").
- **Typical purchase unit [U]:** live birds by kg live weight, in crates or lots; dressed/skinless by kg. Define yield (live to dressed) in the RFQ rather than assume it.
- **Quality specs to request:** live weight range (e.g. state the target, 1.8-2.2 kg is a common retail target [U]), age/withdrawal period after last antibiotic, no visible disease, uniform size, mortality-in-transit allowance, crate hygiene.
- **Seasonality/volatility [U]:** typically volatile; summer heat raises mortality and lowers weight, festival and religious fasting periods shift demand sharply, disease outbreaks (e.g. avian influenza) can move prices abruptly. Plan price-pass-through rules with B2B customers.
- **Slaughter:** chicken processing for retail also requires a compliant source or an own licensed processing unit; confirm with FSSAI/municipality (A5, A6, A8).

### 1.2 Mutton (goat and sheep)
- **Supply [U/LOCAL]:** weekly livestock markets, village traders, farmer groups, dealers who aggregate from Rayalaseema districts. No verified Proddatur or Kadapa livestock market was found. Ask the municipality, Animal Husbandry department and local butchers.
- **Slaughter and legality.** FSSAI Schedule IV Part IV requires meat sold to come from an authorised slaughterhouse; shop-level slaughter is described as illegal in secondary guides, and ante- and post-mortem examination by a veterinary doctor is required, with a local-authority NOC for slaughterhouses [V: FSSAI slaughterhouse checklist; Foodsafetyhelpline and other secondary guides]. Whether Proddatur Municipality runs a functioning slaughterhouse is [LOCAL]. The Proddatur page found only general town information [V: Wikipedia, cdma.ap.gov.in].
- **Purchase unit [U]:** live animal bought per head or per kg live weight, or carcass by kg dressed; define weight basis (live vs carcass, with or without offal/head/skin).
- **Quality specs:** age (young animals), body condition, no visible disease, vet fitness certificate, clear sex/breed labelling (goat vs sheep must not be mixed up), carcass chilled promptly.
- **Seasonality/volatility [U]:** strong demand spikes around festivals (e.g. Eid/Bakrid, Ugadi, Sankranti); prices higher then and supply tight. Launch (10 Jan) falls close to Sankranti; confirm demand and price behaviour with local traders. Summer and drought affect availability.

### 1.3 Fish
- **Supply [U/LOCAL]:** Proddatur is inland; fish arrives from coastal markets or inland freshwater fish farms (Andhra Pradesh is a major aquaculture state). Coastal sources to investigate: Nellore, Chennai, Visakhapatnam. Nothing verified about wholesale market operators or timings.
- **Cold-chain:** ice logistics are the core risk. Define ice-to-fish ratio, insulated boxes and transit time in the RFQ.
- **Purchase unit [U]:** by kg, in ice boxes; by species and size grade.
- **Quality specs:** eyes clear, gills red, firm flesh, no ammonia odour, temperature at receipt near 0-4 C [U], date of catch/harvest, species labelling; freshwater vs sea fish clearly stated.
- **Seasonality [U]:** marine supply drops during the annual fishing ban and monsoon; prices rise then. Confirm dates with the AP Fisheries department [LOCAL].

### 1.4 Prawns (shrimp)
- **Supply:** Nellore district has aquaculture on nearly 60,000 acres across 10 mandals, and usually 90-95% of shrimp is exported, so export demand drives local prices [V: AquaAsia Pacific, Nov 2022; older data, may have changed].
- **Price form [V]:** farm-gate prices are quoted per kg by count size (pieces per kg), and in Nov 2022 prices fell sharply within weeks (the count-50 example dropped from 360 to 270 INR/kg between October and 17 November 2022 [V, historical only]). Do not use these figures for budgeting.
- **Purchase unit:** by kg per count grade (e.g. 30/40/50 count [U]), headless vs head-on, fresh iced vs frozen.
- **Quality specs:** count uniformity, no black spots/soft shell, no sulphite or antibiotic residue claims on paper, harvest date, ice ratio, ideally a lab/residue statement from the supplier [U].
- **Volatility:** high; driven by crop cycle, disease and export markets [V for existence of volatility].

### 1.5 Eggs
- **Rate setting [V].** NECC (National Egg Coordination Committee) sets reference rates at rate-fixation meetings in zonal offices such as Namakkal. Since 1 January 2014 NECC fixes the price farmers receive, and traders add their margin on top [V: The Poultry Site 2013]. Whether rates are now set daily or weekly, and the current level, was not confirmed from a current source [U]; one undated source quoted 6.05 INR per egg at Namakkal farms and should be treated as outdated [V but undated].
- **Supply chain [U]:** Namakkal (Tamil Nadu) is a major hub; Hyderabad/Telangana and Andhra layer belts are alternatives. A local wholesaler in Proddatur/Kadapa will likely quote NECC rate plus freight plus margin. Ask each wholesaler which zone rate they follow.
- **Purchase unit [U]:** trays of 30 eggs; cartons/boxes of multiple trays (confirm count per carton, e.g. 360 or 420, with each supplier).
- **Quality specs:** size grade/weight, shell cleanliness and cracks (breakage allowance in RFQ), date of lay, storage temperature, no off-odour.
- **Seasonality [U]:** demand and price vary; winter higher demand, summer heat lowers laying/shelf life, and festival/religious periods lower demand. Verify with NECC trend reports.

### 1.6 Packaging, labels, equipment, vehicles
- **Packaging [U/LOCAL]:** vacuum pouches, food-grade trays, cartons, insulated boxes, ice packs, labels meeting FSSAI labelling rules. Check label content rules with your FSSAI consultant. Typical sources: IndiaMART listings, Hyderabad/Chennai/Bengaluru packaging dealers.
- **Cold-chain equipment [U/LOCAL]:** walk-in cold room, chest freezers, display chillers, blast chiller, vacuum sealer, generator/inverter. Insist on local service engineer commitment and response time in writing.
- **Reefer/insulated vehicles [U/LOCAL]:** compare buying vs hiring; request temperature log capability; confirm body builder and refrigeration unit warranty and service in Kadapa/Tirupati/Bengaluru/Hyderabad region.
- No dealer names are listed because none were verified.

## 2. Vendor evaluation scorecard

Score each criterion 1-5 (1 poor, 5 excellent). Weighted score = sum(score/5 x weight). Total 100.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What 5 looks like | CSV column |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price stability and rate transparency | 15 | Quotes tied to a named published rate/basis; changes announced in advance | score_price_stability |
| 2 | Quality consistency and spec adherence | 20 | Passes receiving QC on 95%+ of lots in trial | score_quality |
| 3 | Health/vet and statutory documentation | 20 | FSSAI valid, vet certificate per lot, GST, bank proof | score_documentation |
| 4 | Credit terms | 10 | 7+ days credit or weekly settlement | score_credit |
| 5 | Minimum quantity flexibility | 10 | Can supply pilot volumes without large minimums | score_min_qty_flex |
| 6 | Backup capacity | 10 | Can surge or cover another vendor's failure | score_backup_capacity |
| 7 | Location and logistics (distance, delivery time, cold chain) | 15 | Same-day delivery with temperature control | score_location_logistics |

Adjustments:
- **Hard gates (fail = reject regardless of score):** no FSSAI licence/registration where applicable; no vet certificate for meat from slaughter; refuses written spec; cash-only with no invoice.
- Perishables (fish, prawns, chicken): consider raising quality and logistics weights by 5 each, lowering credit and min-qty by 5 each.
- Minimum 2 qualified vendors per category (launch plan). Rank 1 = primary, rank 2 = backup, split e.g. 70/30 [U].
- Re-score monthly using receiving data (rejected_quantity / received_quantity from goods receipts) and on-time delivery.

## 3. RFQ template and onboarding checklist

### 3.1 RFQ template (copy into email/WhatsApp/PDF)

```
RFQ No: DF-RFQ-[category]-[yyyymmdd]            Date: ____
From: DawnFresh, [address], Proddatur, YSR Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh
Contact: [owner name, phone]            Quote due by: ____
1. Product and spec: [product, size/weight range, live/dressed/frozen, grade]
2. Purchase unit and pricing basis: per [kg/tray/piece]; state whether live weight,
   dressed weight or count size; state the published rate you follow, if any, and
   your premium/discount to it.
3. Estimated volume: [per day / per week], pilot volume 3-9 Jan: ____
4. Minimum order quantity and ordering cut-off time: ____
5. Delivery: location, lead time, delivery frequency, who bears ice/crate cost,
   transport temperature at receipt, crate/box return arrangement.
6. Price validity and revision rule (how and when changes are notified).
7. Payment terms: credit days, mode (bank transfer/UPI/cheque), advance if any.
8. GST: registered? GSTIN; GST rate as applied on your invoice.
9. Quality and rejection: rejection criteria, replacement or credit note timing,
   mortality/breakage/shrinkage allowance.
10. Documents attached (see checklist): FSSAI, vet certificate, GST, bank details.
11. Backup capacity: maximum extra quantity per day on 24 h notice.
12. References: two current customers we may contact.
13. Declarations: no banned drug/antibiotic residues; supplies only from
    authorised slaughterhouse/licensed source (for meat).
Signature, name, date: ____
```

## 5. Vendor contact-discovery plan

All lists below are places to look. None are verified vendor contacts.

| Channel | What to do | Product |
|---|---|---|
| Proddatur Municipality (health/sanitary section) | Ask about the licensed slaughter facility, approved butchers, meat-trade rules and local livestock/poultry market days | Mutton, chicken |
| AP Animal Husbandry Dept (district office, Kadapa) | Ask for vet inspection arrangement, slaughter source, livestock market locations | Mutton, chicken |
| AP Fisheries Dept (district office) and MPEDA regional contacts | Aquaculture registrations, hatchery/farm lists, fishing-ban dates | Fish, prawns |
| NECC (Namakkal zonal office, published rates) | Understand rate reference; ask which traders serve Rayalaseema | Eggs |
| Poultry farmers/traders associations (AP/Telangana/Kadapa district) | Ask local butchers and farmers which associations exist; do not list any until confirmed | Chicken, eggs |
| Weekly/daily markets in Proddatur, Kadapa, Jammalamadugu, Kurnool, Tirupati | Visit early morning; note traders, timings, how rates are called, volumes | All |
| Existing butchers and hotels in Proddatur | Ask (politely) who supplies them and what rate basis is used | All |
| Integrator regional/area offices (see names in 1.1) | Enquire about wholesale or bulk dressed-bird supply to retailers | Chicken |
| Wholesale fish markets: Nellore, Chennai, Visakhapatnam | Visit once; talk to commission agents and cold-storage owners; ask about road transport to Proddatur | Fish, prawns |
| IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial | Search by product + "Kadapa", "Proddatur", "Kurnool", "Nellore"; shortlist, then verify FSSAI on FoSCoS and make a site visit; treat listings as leads only | All, packaging, equipment, reefers |
| Dealer fairs/online (packaging and cold-chain equipment makers) | Request quotes from at least 3; ask for service centre location | Packaging, equipment |
| Reefer body builders and commercial vehicle dealers (Tata/Mahindra/Ashok Leyland showrooms in Kadapa/Tirupati/Bengaluru/Hyderabad) | Ask for insulated vs reefer van quotes and approved body builders | Vehicles |
| Local CA and Advisor | Referrals for GST/FSSAI-compliant vendors | All |

Process: (1) build a long list of 5-8 leads per category by 30 Oct; (2) phone screen using the RFQ by 10 Nov; (3) site visit and document check by 15 Nov; (4) trial lot and scoring by 20 Nov (poultry, mutton), 30 Nov (fish, prawns, eggs), 1 Dec (packaging, equipment, transport); (5) sign agreements with primary and backup.

## 6. Open questions for the owner
1. Will DawnFresh slaughter in an own licensed unit, or buy dressed from a municipal/licensed slaughterhouse? This drives mutton and chicken sourcing.
2. Launch product list (are fish and prawns in the pilot?) and target daily volumes per product.
3. Credit capacity: can we offer advance payment to win better prices, and what is the working-capital limit?
4. Is the vehicle bought or hired?
5. Which supplier fields should go into the app (documents, GSTIN, bank, rating)? This needs a schema change.
