## 5. Pricing communication (framework, no prices)

Meat prices follow the daily wholesale market (live bird rate, mutton arrivals, fish landings), so a printed fixed price list would go wrong within days.

Policy framework (for the owner to decide, each item is a decision, not a stated fact):

1. **One daily rate sheet.** Owner or manager sets rates each morning by a cut-off time (e.g. 6 a.m. [E]) from vendor cost plus target margin. A single source avoids staff quoting differently.
2. **Cost-plus rule.** Selling rate = landed cost (vendor rate, yield loss from live to dressed, wastage, packing, delivery) + margin band set by the owner by product. Do not publish margins. Do not call booked order value profit (app convention).
3. **Segmented rates.** Retail walk-in rate, B2B rate, bulk/function rate, franchise supply rate. Never show the B2B rate publicly.
4. **Rate validity window.** Daily rate valid until a stated time; for function bookings, rate fixed at confirmation time or "market-linked, confirmed 24 hours before", a written term.
5. **Price protection.** B2B contract option: weekly rate reviewed on a stated day, change notified by WhatsApp before cut-off.
6. **Weight and billing.** Bill on the actual packed weight on the stamped scale; the quotation message in `/shop` already says final weight and price are confirmed after.
7. **Credit terms.** Plan decision 6 is open. Suggest cash/UPI on delivery for new B2B accounts in the first month, credit by owner approval only, with a limit and days stated in writing [E recommendation].
8. **Introductory offers.** Owner defines the form (e.g. first delivery sample, free delivery for first week, referral). Avoid "lowest price in Proddatur" (cannot be proven).
9. **Public communication.** Say "Today's rate on WhatsApp" and show the dated rate board in the outlet. Telugu: "నేటి రేటు వాట్సాప్‌లో".
10. **Rate-change log.** Record the daily rate sheet in the app (or a dated sheet) so disputes can be resolved. Rates in the app's product catalog are only entered by the owner at configuration (F7).
11. **Competitor watch.** Weekly note of 5-6 competitor rates for internal use only.

## 7. Franchise recruitment pitch outline (use after pilot data, Feb onward)

Do not circulate before there is real pilot margin data (Plan risk table). No income promises; show only measured results and ranges with assumptions.

1. **Who we are**: brand, company unit, licences, leadership, pilot location.
2. **The problem in the territory**: unhygienic or inconsistent supply, weight disputes, no cold chain (support with our own competitor map, not hearsay).
3. **The model**: franchise has one allocated location with an admin-set radius of 1-50 km (default 20); company supplies from the central unit; franchisee gets their own login in the operations workspace.
4. **What we provide**: supply, SOPs, training, branding, software (orders, leads, supply requests, stock), lead generation help in the franchise radius (territory discovery, max 20 per search).
5. **What the franchisee provides**: premises, licences (their own FSSAI), staff, working capital, compliance with SOPs.
6. **Pilot results** (real numbers only): orders/day, average order value, wastage, gross margin range, unpaid ratio, complaint rate, repeat rate. Label as company pilot, location-specific.
7. **Unit economics template**: investment, fit-out, working capital, fees/royalty (owner decision; do not state before decided), supply pricing mechanism per section 5, break-even scenarios with explicit assumptions. Mark as illustrative.
8. **Territory protection and exclusivity**, minimum performance, term, renewal, exit.
9. **Support model**: onboarding timeline (training, dry run, soft launch), field visits, weekly KPI review.
10. **Quality and brand control**: audits, temperature logs, complaint handling, termination for hygiene breach.
11. **Selection criteria**: local standing, capital, premises, food-business experience, commitment.
12. **Process and timeline**: enquiry, meeting, territory check, agreement, licences, training, opening.
13. **Legal and disclosures**: agreement drafted by a lawyer; check that franchise disclosure expectations are met [U; India has no single franchise statute, advisor to confirm]; no guaranteed returns.
14. **Next step and contact.**

Prerequisites: entity structure (LLP or Pvt Ltd favoured), trademark filing (A13), agreement draft, final brand name.
