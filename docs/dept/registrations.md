## 2. Order of operations (what unlocks what)

1. **Decide entity (A1) by 25 Oct.** Everything else is filed in the entity's name. Lease, FSSAI, GST and bank all must match.
2. **PAN and bank account (A2).** Needed for GST, Udyam, FSSAI payment and most others.
3. **Premises shortlist, zoning check with the Municipality, then lease (B2/B3).** FSSAI, trade licence, fire and PCB all need an address and proof of possession. Check zoning before signing the lease.
4. **GST (A3) and Udyam (A4)** in parallel once PAN and address proof exist.
5. **FSSAI (A5) as soon as lease and entity exist.** Start first; the plan's own critical path says it is the longest lead item. Applications for the processing unit and the outlet are separate.
6. **Municipal trade and meat licence (A6) and AP Shops and Establishments (A7).** There is a 2020 AP notification on a single form for S&E registration and a provisional trade licence in municipal systems (see source S5), so ask the Municipality whether both can be filed together.
7. **Vet and slaughter source (A8)** before the processing dry run (21 Dec).
8. **APPCB (A9) and Fire (A10)** only after confirming applicability. APPCB Consent for Establishment, if required, must precede construction/fit-out, so decide this before fit-out starts on 25 Nov.
9. **Legal Metrology (A11)** after scales are purchased; before first sale.
10. **PT / ESI / EPF (A12)** at first hire (target 1 Dec for the manager).
11. **Trademark (A13) and insurance (A14)** are not legal prerequisites to open, but file the trademark early so signage is not wasted.

Legal gating before first sale (best understanding, UNVERIFIED for local practice): FSSAI licence or registration, trade/meat licence, and verified scales. Do not sell on an acknowledgement alone; the launch plan also says this.

## 3. Registration by registration

### A1. Entity choice
- **Applies:** Yes.
- **Authority:** Ministry of Corporate Affairs for LLP or Pvt Ltd; proprietorship needs no incorporation.
- **Options (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED):** Proprietorship is fastest and cheapest but has unlimited liability and is weak for a franchisor. LLP or Pvt Ltd gives limited liability and a cleaner entity to sign franchise agreements, hold the trademark and take investment. Pvt Ltd has heavier annual compliance (audit, board meetings, ROC filings); LLP is lighter.
- **Recommendation for owner and CA to confirm:** If franchising is a real goal, choose LLP or Pvt Ltd. If the owner wants to open on 10 Jan with minimum friction and move to a company later, note that licences (FSSAI, trade, S&E) are issued per legal entity and would need to be re-applied or transferred on a later change.
- **Timeline:** Not verified. Company or LLP incorporation commonly takes some days to a few weeks (UNVERIFIED). Given 25 Oct decision date, incorporate by about mid Nov at the latest.
- **Documents:** PAN, Aadhaar, address proof and photo of each director/partner; registered-office proof (utility bill plus owner NOC or rent agreement); DSC for directors (company/LLP).
- **Source:** https://www.mca.gov.in (not fetched; UNVERIFIED).

### A2. PAN and current bank account
- **Applies:** Yes.
- **Prerequisites:** Entity incorporation certificate (or proprietor's personal PAN).
- **Documents (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED):** Incorporation documents, entity PAN, KYC of signatories, address proof, board resolution or partner authority letter, specimen signatures.
- **Timeline:** Entity PAN is normally issued with incorporation for company/LLP (UNVERIFIED). Current account opening depends on the bank.
- **Note:** Open the account before GST, because GST registration asks for bank details (may be within 30 days after registration; UNVERIFIED).

### A3. GST

**Is registration required?** Mandatory if aggregate turnover crosses the threshold, or for inter-state supply of taxable goods, or e-commerce supply (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED; check current threshold at gst.gov.in). Important nuance: if every item DawnFresh sells is nil-rated or exempt (fresh meat, fish, eggs), registration may not be legally compulsory below threshold. But the business will also supply packaged/branded or processed items and will want input tax credit on equipment, rent and services, and B2B customers (hotels, caterers) usually expect a GSTIN. Franchise royalty or fees are a taxable service. Ask the CA. Plan to register by 15 Nov as in the launch plan.

**Rate treatment (from search results; all UNVERIFIED against the notification text):**

| Product | What search results said | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh or chilled meat (HSN 0201, 0203, 0207 etc.) | Nil rate under Notification 10/2025-Central Tax (Rate), effective 22 Sep 2025 per a West Bengal state rate chart (source S2) | Medium; one secondary source disagreed (said 5%) |
| Frozen meat, unbranded/not pre-packaged and labelled | Same chart lists "other than fresh or chilled, other than pre-packaged and labelled" as nil | Low-Medium |
| Pre-packaged and labelled / branded frozen meat | Rate not confirmed. Older sources said 5%; one said unbranded frozen unit-container 12%. | UNVERIFIED. CA must read the notification. |
| Fresh or chilled fish (0302) | Nil per the same chart | Medium |
| Frozen or packed fish and prawns | Not confirmed (older sources: 5% or 12%) | UNVERIFIED |
| Eggs in shell (HSN 0407) | 0% / exempt per a HSN lookup site and a tax-software guide (source S3). Processed egg products differ and were not checked. | Low-Medium |

Context: GST rates were restructured with effect from 22 Sep 2025 (Notification 9/2025-Central Tax (Rate) for taxable goods). The 2026 chart cited in S2 is dated 16 Apr 2026, so later amendments between then and Jan 2027 are possible.

**Practical consequence for DawnFresh:** The distinction that matters is fresh/chilled (nil, apparently) versus pre-packaged, labelled, branded (possibly taxable). Vacuum-packed, labelled retail packs may fall on the taxable side. Decide product and packaging format with the CA before label printing.

- **Authority:** GST portal. **Documents (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED):** PAN, Aadhaar, incorporation proof, address proof of business premises (rent agreement and owner NOC), bank proof, photo, authorised signatory proof. Separate GST registration for each state; multiple outlets in AP can be added as additional places of business (confirm with CA).
- **Timeline:** Not verified; commonly several working days after application with Aadhaar authentication (UNVERIFIED).
- **Fees:** No government fee (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED).
- **Sources:** S2 (rate chart), S1b (taxguru), https://www.gst.gov.in.

### A4. Udyam (MSME)
- **Applies:** Optional but recommended; helps with bank credit and some schemes.
- **Authority:** Ministry of MSME, udyamregistration.gov.in.
- **Documents:** Aadhaar of proprietor/authorised signatory, PAN, GSTIN (if any), bank details, NIC activity codes, investment/turnover figures.
- **Fees/timeline:** Understood to be free and online, often completed same day (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED; not checked in this session). Beware of paid "agents".
- **Prerequisites:** PAN (and GST if registered).

### A5. FSSAI licensing

**Tier structure (consistent across search results, source S1, UNVERIFIED against regulation text):**
- Basic registration: turnover up to Rs 12 lakh. Fee Rs 100 a year (secondary source).
- State licence: turnover about Rs 12 lakh to Rs 20 crore. Fee seen Rs 2,000 to 5,000 a year (secondary source).
- Central licence: above Rs 20 crore turnover, operations in more than one state, or capacity above set limits.
- Meat-specific capacity limits quoted in search results: slaughterhouse central licence above 50 large animals, 150 small animals or 1,000 poultry a day; meat processing central licence above 500 kg a day or 150 MT a year. A 2020 FSSAI eligibility document is cited for these but could not be fetched; treat as UNVERIFIED.
- A 2020 FSSAI document reportedly gives a meat shop fee of Rs 2,000 (state) / Rs 7,500 (central). UNVERIFIED.

**Application to DawnFresh (provisional, confirm in FoSCoS category selector):**

| Premises | Likely category | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Processing / cold-storage unit (cutting, grinding, vacuum packing, freezing) | State licence for meat processing (and/or storage). Not central unless above 500 kg/day or above 150 MT/year | Small pilot volumes; single state |
| Retail outlet (raw meat counter) | State licence or basic registration depending on turnover and FoSCoS category for meat shop | Meat shops appear as their own category; whether turnover-based registration is accepted for them is UNVERIFIED |
| Franchise outlets | Each outlet needs its own FSSAI licence/registration in the **franchisee's** name (or the operator's), at that outlet's address. The central/state tier is tested per licensee. | General knowledge, UNVERIFIED |
| Delivery to hotels/caterers | Covered by the processing/distribution licence if the same premises supplies; confirm whether a separate "distributor/transporter" category is needed | UNVERIFIED |
| Any on-site slaughter | Slaughterhouse category applies. The plan assumes slaughter happens at a municipal or licensed unit, not in-house. Keeping slaughter outside DawnFresh avoids this category. | Design decision |

- **Documents (typical, UNVERIFIED):** Photo ID, entity documents, premises proof (lease plus NOC), list of food categories, layout/plan of the processing unit, equipment list, water test report (potability), medical fitness certificates for food handlers, Food Safety Management System plan (for licence), municipal/trade licence copy where required, supplier list.
- **Prerequisites:** Entity, premises with lease. Hygiene, drainage, separation of raw and cooked areas must meet Schedule 4 requirements before inspection (UNVERIFIED).
- **Timeline:** The plan assumes apply by 20 Nov and issue by 15 Dec. Statutory processing timelines were not verified. Inspection may be required for a licence and a meat shop licence involves facility inspection (source S6). Add buffer.
- **Authority and portal:** FSSAI, FoSCoS, foscos.fssai.gov.in; local Designated Officer under the Commissioner of Food Safety, AP.
- **Labelling:** Packaged products need FSSAI-compliant labels with licence number. Legal Metrology packaged-commodity rules also apply (see A11).
- **Sources:** S1, S6, https://foscos.fssai.gov.in.

### A6. Proddatur Municipality: trade licence and meat licence
- **Applies:** Yes for the retail outlet and the processing unit (trade licence); meat shop/slaughter permission under the municipal law.
- **Legal basis:** The Andhra Pradesh Municipalities Act, 1965 provides for licences for slaughterhouses, slaughter of animals for sale as food, butchers, fishmongers and poulterers (UNEP summary, source S7). Exact sections and current procedure UNVERIFIED.
- **Process:** A 2020 AP notification set up a single form for S&E registration and a provisional trade licence in municipal systems (source S5). Ask the Municipal Commissioner's office whether meat licence is a separate application and whether the Municipal Health Officer inspects the premises.
- **Fees:** A loan-marketing blog says Rs 1,000 to 5,000 a year for municipal licence (source S8). UNVERIFIED and not specific to Proddatur.
- **Documents (typical, UNVERIFIED):** Lease and landlord NOC, ID, entity documents, property tax receipt, FSSAI copy or application, zoning/land-use confirmation, photos or plan, possibly NOC from neighbours.
- **Prerequisites:** Lease signed; check zoning first. Meat shops may be restricted to specified zones or distances from places of worship and homes (general practice, UNVERIFIED for Proddatur).
- **Timeline:** Not verified. Plan target: apply by 20 Nov.
- **Source to check:** Proddatur Municipality office; AP Municipal Administration Department (cdma.ap.gov.in, not fetched).

### A7. AP Shops and Establishments
- **Applies:** Yes for each outlet and the processing unit's office/establishment, even with one employee (source S4).
- **Authority:** AP Labour Department, under the AP Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1988.
- **Process:** Integrated Form A within 30 days of starting business; fee is a slab by number of employees, paid online via MeeSeva (source S4). Renewal now through auto-renewal by employer self-certification (source S4). A single online form is reported to give instant registration and a provisional trade licence (source S5).
- **Fees:** Rs 125 to 500 per a blog (UNVERIFIED).
- **Documents (typical, UNVERIFIED):** Entity proof, ID, address and lease proof, employee count, photos.
- **Timeline:** Instant online per S5 (UNVERIFIED). Plan target 1 Dec. Because the 30-day clock runs from commencement, filing at or before opening is safe.
- **Sources:** S4, S5.

### A8. Veterinary and slaughter source
- **Applies:** Yes for mutton (and chicken if birds are dressed in-house). Buying from a licensed slaughterhouse or municipal slaughterhouse keeps DawnFresh out of the slaughterhouse licence category.
- **Authority:** Municipal Health Officer (slaughterhouse licence, inspection powers under the Municipalities Act, source S7); Animal Husbandry Department veterinary officer for ante-mortem and post-mortem certification.
- **What is not verified:** The AP-specific form and certificate name, who issues it in Proddatur, whether Proddatur has a functioning municipal slaughterhouse, and fees. A Tamil Nadu order illustrates a Form 4 fitness certificate by a veterinary assistant surgeon (not AP).
- **Action:** Visit the Proddatur Municipal Health Officer and the Assistant Director, Animal Husbandry, Proddatur/Kadapa. Get written confirmation of (a) approved slaughter source, (b) certification routine per batch, (c) waste disposal route. Collect copies of each supplier's licence and vet certificates for the FSSAI file.
- **Poultry:** Live-bird shops and dressing are regulated by the municipality and FSSAI; if DawnFresh buys dressed or live birds from integrators or traders, request their FSSAI licence numbers.

### A9. APPCB consent
- **Applies:** Uncertain. Orange-category units need Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate (source S9, a shrimp-processing case). Whether a small meat processing/cold-storage unit or a retail meat shop is Red, Orange, Green or exempt in APPCB's list was **not found**. Slaughterhouses are normally treated as a pollution category in CPCB lists (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED).
- **Action:** Write to or visit the APPCB Regional Office (Kadapa/Kurnool region; UNVERIFIED) with the activity description and ask for the written category. If a CFE is required, it must come before fit-out.
- **Timeline:** A consultancy lists 7 days (Green), 15 (Orange), 21 (Red) (UNVERIFIED).
- **Documents (typical):** Land/lease proof, project report, layout, water and effluent details, entity documents, fee.
- **Source:** S9; official: https://pcb.ap.gov.in (not fetched).

### A10. Fire safety NOC
- **Applies:** Statute reportedly triggers an NOC for commercial buildings above 15 m, and public-assembly buildings above 500 sq m plot or 6 m high (AP Fire Service Amendment Act 2006 per source S10). A single-storey outlet or small ground-floor unit is likely below this, but cold rooms, ammonia refrigeration and generator fuel can bring additional safety requirements (UNVERIFIED). 2017 AP Building Rules text conflicts on heights; current threshold UNVERIFIED.
- **Action:** Ask the landlord whether the building already has a fire NOC (common for commercial complexes). Obtain a copy for the file. Install extinguishers and basic fire safety regardless.
- **Authority:** AP State Disaster Response and Fire Services Department.
- **Timeline and fee:** UNVERIFIED.

### A11. Legal Metrology
- **Applies:** Yes. Weighing scales used in trade must be verified and stamped before use (Legal Metrology Act 2009 section 24, source S11). Also applies to packaged commodities (declarations on labels: net quantity, MRP, date, consumer care) for vacuum packs; requirement for dealers/packers registration under Packaged Commodities Rules is UNVERIFIED here.
- **Authority:** AP Legal Metrology Department (under Consumer Affairs, Food and Civil Supplies). A draft May 2026 AP rule would allow approved third-party test centres to verify weights and measures (still a draft, source S11).
- **Fees, re-verification period (commonly annual):** UNVERIFIED for AP.
- **Process:** Buy scales from a Legal Metrology licensed dealer/manufacturer (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED); present for verification and stamp; keep certificate at the outlet. Plan target 20 Dec; do it after the scales arrive.
- **Source:** S11; official: AP Legal Metrology department site (not fetched).

### A12. Professional tax, ESI, EPF
- **Professional tax (sourced S12, secondary sites):** Nil up to Rs 15,000 monthly salary, Rs 150 for 15,001-20,000, Rs 200 above 20,000. Annual cap Rs 2,500. Employers get a PTRC (to deduct from employees) and a PTEC (own liability) via the AP Commercial Taxes portal. Due date reported as 10th-15th of the following month (conflicting). Confirm slab and date on the portal. Apply at first hire.
- **ESI (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED):** Usually applies at 10 or more employees in a notified area with wage ceiling Rs 21,000 a month. Check current rule at esic.gov.in.
- **EPF (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED):** Mandatory at 20 or more employees; voluntary coverage possible earlier. Check epfindia.gov.in.
- **Pilot headcount per launch plan:** about 8-11 people (manager 1, butchers 2-3, helpers 2, counter 1-2, drivers 1-2), so ESI may apply and EPF probably not at first. Count contract/outsourced staff according to rules (UNVERIFIED). CA to confirm.
- **Other labour items to ask the CA about:** Payment of wages, minimum wages notification for AP shops and establishments, gratuity (applies at 10 or more employees, UNVERIFIED), and food-handler medical fitness records (required by FSSAI).

### A13. Trademark
- **Applies:** Recommended. Needed for franchising, since the franchisor must own the brand it licenses.
- **Authority:** Trade Marks Registry, ipindia.gov.in.
- **Steps:** Final brand name (E1 by 5 Nov), public search of the register, file in the correct class(es) (likely Class 29 for meat and eggs, Class 35 for retail services, Class 43 if cooked food; class selection UNVERIFIED), under the entity name (not the owner's personal name if entity chosen).
- **Fees/timeline:** Not verified. Registration usually takes many months; filing gives "TM" use rights immediately (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED). Registration will not complete by 10 Jan; the goal is an acknowledged filing.
- **Note:** The name "DawnFresh" must be searched before signage; the plan assumes the brand is not final.

### A14. Insurance
- **Applies:** Not a statutory prerequisite for opening except motor third-party for vehicles (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED). Strongly advisable for stock and cold-chain loss, fire and burglary on premises, product liability, public liability, employee compensation or group cover.
- **Fees/timeline:** UNVERIFIED; get 2-3 broker quotes by 15 Dec. Cold-chain spoilage cover often has exclusions for power failure and for unmaintained equipment (general knowledge); read the policy wording.
- **Prerequisites:** Premises address, stock value estimate, vehicle registration, equipment list.

## 4. Franchise agreement key-terms checklist (not legal advice; have a lawyer draft)

Note on compliance: FSSAI licences must be held by whoever operates each outlet; the franchise agreement should state who. Franchise fees and royalties attract GST; ask the CA.

1. Parties and structure: franchisor entity (name, CIN/LLPIN), franchisee entity or individual, guarantors.
2. Grant: brand, trademark licence (confirm trademark filing status first), system and know-how licence; non-exclusive or exclusive; no transfer or sub-franchise without consent.
3. Territory: location address, working radius (product default 20 km, range 1-50 km), exclusivity rules, online-order allocation inside the territory, company-direct customers in the territory.
4. Term, renewal, and conditions for renewal.
5. Fees: initial franchise fee, refundability, royalty basis (percent of sales or fixed), marketing fund, technology fee, payment dates, late interest, GST.
6. Supply: mandatory purchase from the company, price basis, order and payment terms (franchise supply is requested and priced by the company), minimum orders, delivery schedule, shortages and force majeure, credit limit and security deposit.
7. Pricing and promotions: maximum retail price controls (legal advice needed on competition law), company-mandated promotions.
8. Quality and food safety: franchisee's own FSSAI licence, hygiene SOPs, temperature logs, medical fitness of staff, cold chain, product recall, audit and inspection rights, secret shopper.
9. Compliance responsibilities: municipal trade and meat licence, S&E, scales stamped, GST, labour laws; who bears penalties; franchisee must provide copies of licences and renewals.
10. Software and data: use of the FreshRoute workspace, accurate stock and sales entry, settlement based on recorded supply, sales and collections, data ownership, access removal on termination, privacy.
11. Reporting: daily close, stock reconciliation, wastage rules, audit access to books.
12. Brand standards: signage, uniforms, store layout, approved packaging, social media rules.
13. Training and support: initial training (duration, place), ongoing support, operations manual.
14. Premises: lease in franchisee's name, landlord consent to assignment, step-in rights for franchisor.
15. Insurance: required covers and franchisor named as additional insured.
16. Capital: franchisee's investment responsibility; no income or profit guarantees (do not promise returns before pilot data exists).
17. Intellectual property: ownership of brand, customer lists, improvements; non-compete and non-solicit during term and after (enforceability limits post-term; lawyer to advise).
18. Indemnity and liability: product liability, customer claims, regulatory penalties, caps.
19. Termination: grounds (non-payment, safety breach, licence loss), cure periods, de-branding, inventory buy-back, handover of customers and phone numbers, outstanding payment recovery.
20. Transfer and exit: franchisee's sale of business, right of first refusal, death or incapacity.
21. Dispute resolution: governing law, arbitration seat and rules, interim relief, jurisdiction.
22. Franchisee disclosure document: provide pilot financials, fee list and litigation history before signing (India has no specific franchise statute; UNVERIFIED, lawyer to confirm). Cooling-off period.
23. Stamp duty and registration of the agreement under AP stamp rules (UNVERIFIED amounts).

## 6. Risks to the 10 Jan 2027 date

1. **Licence lead times unverified.** FSSAI and municipal timelines were not confirmed; FSSAI inspection, if required, falls in mid-Dec. Apply by 20 Nov as planned and have the premises layout ready earlier.
2. **Lease signature (25 Nov) is a hard gate** for FSSAI, trade licence, S&E, fire and PCB. Any slippage cascades.
3. **Zoning refusal for meat trade.** Check with the Municipality before signing.
4. **APPCB applicability unresolved.** If a Consent to Establish is needed, it must come before fit-out and could delay the unit.
5. **Unknown slaughter and vet arrangement in Proddatur.** Mutton supply depends on it.
6. **GST on packaged and frozen products unconfirmed**, which affects labelling, pricing and invoicing software settings.
7. **Entity decision (25 Oct)** is on the critical path; a delay pushes everything.
8. **Trademark** will not be registered by launch; name conflict risk if the brand is not searched.
9. **Cold chain and power**; not a registration item but insurance and licence inspections depend on it.
10. **Legal Metrology** verification slot at the district office may take time; book early.
11. **Rules may change.** AP draft Legal Metrology rules (May 2026) and GST amendments after Apr 2026 may affect procedures.

## 7. Open questions for the owner

1. Which entity (proprietorship, LLP, Pvt Ltd)? Final brand name?
2. Will any slaughter happen in-house, or will all mutton and poultry arrive from licensed units?
3. Exact premises and area (sq m) and number of floors? Is it in a commercial complex with an existing fire NOC?
4. Will you sell vacuum-packed, labelled or branded frozen products at launch, or only fresh/chilled and loose?
5. Expected first-year turnover and daily kilograms (decides FSSAI tier and GST threshold)?
6. Will the first franchise be a separate legal entity with its own FSSAI licence?
7. How many staff at launch (decides ESI, EPF and PT)?
8. Who will file: you, the CA, or a local consultant? Who holds the FoSCoS login?

10. Do you already hold a PAN-linked bank account or any existing business registration?
