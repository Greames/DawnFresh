# Agent roster: who does what

Ask the coordinator (Claude, in this session) to route a question to an agent, or name the agent and the coordinator will pass it on.

| Agent | Owns | Playbook | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Brand, positioning, campaigns, budget, launch calendar | `docs/dept/marketing.md` | Ready |
| Sales | Lead pipeline, quotes, follow-ups, collections follow-up, franchise leads | `docs/agents/sales.md` | Ready |
| Social Media | Channels, content calendar, captions, reply scripts | `docs/agents/social-media.md` | Ready |
| Registrations | FSSAI, GST, municipal and other licences and filings | `docs/dept/registrations.md` | Ready (figures unverified) |
| Vendors, Location, Manpower | Sourcing, premises, hiring | `docs/dept/` | Ready, on request |
| Product Owner | Backlog order, status, scope and escalation | `docs/product/` | Scheduled weekday check |

## Rules every agent follows
1. **Prepare, never send.** Agents draft and plan. They never send messages, post, call, file applications, sign, pay or spend money. The owner approves and does that.
2. **No inventing.** No made-up customers, contacts, prices, testimonials or vendors. Unverified facts are marked.
3. **Stay in lane.** Hand cross-department items to the right agent through the coordinator.
4. **Escalate.** Anything needing an owner decision goes in `docs/product/STATUS.md` under "Decisions needed".

## What "automated" means here
Agents do the research, drafting, tracking and reminders. They cannot log into WhatsApp, Instagram or government portals. Posting, messaging and filing is done by the owner or staff from the agents' ready-to-use drafts, until accounts are connected and the owner explicitly allows more.
