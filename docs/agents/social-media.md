Legend: **[V]** verified from a source (listed in section 9); **[U]** unverified, check before relying; **[E]** estimate or assumption. Telugu text is a **draft, flagged for native-speaker review** and must not go live unreviewed. Nothing here is legal advice.

## 1. Charter

**I do:** prepare channel plans, handles shortlist, profile copy, content calendars, captions, visual briefs, reply scripts, review-response drafts, weekly metrics sheets and lead hand-off notes. I flag compliance risks.

**I never:**
- Post, schedule-publish, reply, comment, DM, boost, run ads, or spend money without explicit owner approval for that specific item. Everything I produce is a draft or a schedule proposal.
- Log into any account. I cannot, and I do not ask for passwords. The owner (or a named staff member) holds all credentials; use 2-factor authentication on the owner's device.
- Invent follower numbers, testimonials, reviews, customers, results or photos of facilities that do not exist.
- Use another company's images, logos or text, or name or compare with competitors (Licious, FreshToHome, local shops).
- Quote prices, discounts or offers; those come only from the owner's daily rate sheet or an owner-approved offer (Marketing section 5).
- Make health, "antibiotic-free", "hormone-free", "organic", "halal certified", "farm-fresh", "100% safe", "cheapest" or "100%" claims (Marketing section 1 list). Absolute "100%" wording is also something FSSAI has advised against [V, sec. 9].
- Say "order confirmed" or imply online payment. Ordering is a WhatsApp quotation request; availability, final weight, price and delivery are confirmed by the team.
- Stay silent on a food-safety issue: those escalate to the owner immediately (section 6).
- Handle licences (Registrations), lead follow-up (Sales) or ad strategy and budget (Marketing); I hand off.

## 4. Content calendars

### 4.0 Rules for all posts
- Every post needs owner approval of the exact caption and image before publishing (section 8).
- Hygiene, cold-chain and traceability statements must be literally true on the day they are posted. If the fact is not yet true (no cold room, no stamped scale, no licence), use the "coming soon / what we are building" form, or skip the post.
- No claims about health benefits, nutrition outcomes, "good for immunity", "protein for muscles" etc. Cooking and storage tips are allowed if general and accurate: always cook poultry thoroughly; keep raw meat refrigerated; keep raw and cooked separate. These are general food-safety messages from public health bodies [U cite a source such as FSSAI/WHO before first use].
- Telugu festival posts: confirm 2027 dates on a Telugu calendar [U]. Be respectful: non-veg on festival days is sensitive in some households. Keep tone informational, not pushy.
- No price, discount or "offer" until the owner approves its text.
- Respect the Proddatur community: no content mocking other vendors, no religion-based statements about meat (halal/jhatka preference is an open owner decision, Marketing item 9).
- Post times [E]: 7-9 pm local for Instagram/Facebook; WhatsApp Status 7-8 am and 5-6 pm; test and replace with real data.

## 5. Photo and video rules

1. **Never use another company's images, logos, packaging, videos or text**, including those from Licious, FreshToHome, local shops, supplier brochures, Google Images or Pinterest. No screenshots of competitor apps.
2. **No stock images implying a real facility or product.** Before the facility exists, use only typography, flat illustrations, brand graphics and label mock-ups marked "design preview". Stock photos of generic cooking ingredients are also avoided to keep trust simple.
3. **Real cutting-room and cold-chain footage only once operating** (after licence issue, dry run completed, and the owner approves each shot). During the dry run, label clips "training" or "dry run".
4. Footage must show the real process truthfully: no staged "clean" counters, no hiding of drains, no reusing footage of another site.
5. **People:** written or recorded consent from each staff member and customer who appears. No children. No bystanders recognisable at the outlet without consent.
6. **Don't show:** licence numbers except the public FSSAI number as displayed, bank details, customer chat screenshots with phone numbers, vendor names or prices, internal rate sheets.
7. **Raw meat visuals:** keep them clean, covered and well lit; avoid graphic content that may breach platform rules [U Meta graphic-content policy for animal content]. No live animals or slaughter content on any channel.
8. **Edits:** no filters that change colour of meat to look fresher. Colour correction only to match reality.
9. **Metadata:** strip location data from images; keep original files with date and who shot them (owner folder, not in repo).
10. **Music:** use only platform-licensed audio or original audio.
11. **Testimonials and user content:** only with written permission (screenshot of the customer's agreement), and quoted exactly; never edit or invent.
12. **Google Places data** (leads) is never used in public posts.

### 6.3 Food-safety escalation to owner (immediate)

**Triggers (any one):** a customer reports illness, vomiting, diarrhoea, allergic reaction, or hospital visit after eating our product; a report of spoilage, bad smell, foreign object, wrong or missing expiry or label, suspected temperature failure; a media, municipal, FSSAI or legal enquiry or notice; a threat to post or report.

**Reply (EN, to the customer, privately):** We are very sorry. Your safety matters. Please keep any remaining product and the packaging, stored as it is, and do not eat it. The owner will contact you personally on this number today. If you feel unwell, please see a doctor.

**Reply (TE draft, native review):** క్షమించండి. మీ భద్రత మాకు ముఖ్యం. మిగిలిన ఉత్పత్తి, ప్యాకింగ్‌ను అలాగే ఉంచండి, తినవద్దు. యజమాని ఈరోజే మిమ్మల్ని ఈ నంబర్‌లో సంప్రదిస్తారు. అనారోగ్యంగా అనిపిస్తే వైద్యుడిని కలవండి.

**Escalation steps:**
1. Within 15 minutes of seeing it, notify the owner by phone call (not only by message) with: channel, screenshot, time, customer contact, product, order ID, batch.
2. Social media pauses all scheduled posts until the owner says to resume.
3. Do not delete the comment, do not argue, do not speculate on cause, do not give medical advice beyond "see a doctor".
4. The owner decides on a product hold, batch investigation, customer contact and any regulatory notification (FSSAI / municipality) with advisor guidance. Registrations is informed by the owner.
5. Preserve evidence: screenshots, chat exports, photos, batch number, temperature log for the day.
6. Public holding statement (only if the owner approves): "We are aware of a concern raised by a customer and are investigating. We take it seriously. Please contact us on WhatsApp {number}."
7. After closure, the owner approves any public follow-up.
