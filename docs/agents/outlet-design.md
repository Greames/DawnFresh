## 1. Charter

**What I do:** prepare designs, specifications, checklists, cost models, fabricator/architect briefs, route-planning methods and decision tables for (1) a repeatable, franchise-ready physical retail outlet and (2) a mobile outlet (vehicle, trailer or cart selling from stops or routes).

**What I never do:** contract, pay, order, apply for any licence or permission, sign, or contact any vendor, official, landlord or fabricator. I name vendor categories only, never vendors, and I invent no prices, contacts or quotes. The owner approves and acts. Anything needing an owner decision is listed in sections 6 and 7.

**Lane limits:** premises search, processing-unit layout and power for the unit belong to Location and infrastructure. Licence filing belongs to Registrations. Brand name, logo and claims belong to Marketing. Software changes belong to the engineering backlog (issues #6-#20 in Greames/DawnFresh); I only propose new items (section 8).

**Working assumptions** (change any of them and the numbers move):
- A1. The processing unit (location-outlet.md) dresses and cuts. Outlets and vans receive cut-ready, packed or carcass product and do only cut-to-order, weigh, pack and sell. **No live-bird slaughter at any outlet or van.** This keeps waste, water, municipal and licence burden low and makes the format repeatable. If the owner wants live-bird dressing at outlets, this whole pack needs a rework (see section 7, Q1).
- A2. Products: chicken (lead), mutton, eggs, fish, prawns (as in the app catalogue).
- A3. Blended realisation about Rs 250/kg across meat lines [E]; gross margin after wastage 12-20% of sales [E]. Both must be replaced by pilot data. (A 2020 Karnataka civic mobile meat van sold chicken at Rs 220/kg [V, https://www.deccanherald.com/state/hubballi-dharwad/coronavirus-lockdown-hubballi-civic-body-launches-meat-on-wheels-830410.html]; that is a dated, different-city reference, not a Proddatur price.)

### 2.3 Zoning and flow (all formats)

Zones: R receive/hang, C cold storage, X raw cutting, P pack/label, D display and sale, B billing, W wash and hand-wash, T waste, S staff and chemicals. One-way flow: dirty to clean, raw to sale, no backtracking through the customer area.

Rules (design intent, confirm against Schedule 4 text [U]):
- Receiving through a rear or side door, never through the customer area. Crates off the floor, clean crates only inside.
- Customers never enter the cutting zone: counter plus a glass or sneeze barrier, 90 cm counter height [E].
- Hand-wash basin (foot or knee tap, soap, paper towel) visible to customers and one at the cutting zone [E, count to confirm].
- Floor: impervious, anti-skid tile, slope 1:50 [E] to a trapped, screened floor drain. Walls tiled to at least 2 m (location-outlet.md uses 2 m or more; FSSAI references 1 m for poultry [U]). Coved corners.
- Separate chopping boards or colour coding: red raw meat, blue fish and prawn, yellow poultry [E practice]. Eggs on their own shelf away from raw meat.
- Waste: closed foot-pedal bins inside; one lidded outside bin on a washable pad; daily pickup arranged by Location agent (B8).
- Staff changing: lockers and a toilet in the premises, not opening onto the cutting zone. Kiosk may use a shared staff toilet [U, confirm].
- Lighting at least 220 lux in work zones, 540 lux at inspection points per location-outlet.md citing FSSAI audit material [U for retail]; use cool-white (4000-5000 K) at work, and neutral-white (about 3000-4000 K) over display so meat looks natural. Avoid pink "meat lights" that mask colour (misleading presentation; [E]).
- Insect screens and an electric fly killer positioned away from open product [E]. Pest control contract through Location agent.

### 2.6 Display, hygiene and customer-facing requirements

- Display chiller 0-4 C with a customer-visible digital thermometer; log temperature at open, midday and close [E practice; targets per location-outlet.md, regulation to confirm U].
- Meat shown in clean SS or food-grade trays, labelled by product and cut. No hanging carcasses at street side, no uncovered open stock on the counter overnight.
- Eggs on a dry shelf away from raw meat. Fish and prawn on crushed ice in their own tray, with ice drain.
- Prices: dated daily rate board, in line with marketing.md section 5 (the board shows only the price that was set). Telugu first, English second (marketing.md).
- Staff: clean apron, hair cover, gloves for ready-to-sell handling, closed footwear; medical fitness records and FoSTaC training for the supervisor [U, check requirement with Registrations/Manpower].
- Cleaning: opening checklist (temperatures, hand-wash, sanitiser), mid-shift wipe-down, closing deep clean; one signed checklist per day, kept 1 year [E]. Pest and chemical store separate.
- Scales: face the customer so the reading is visible; tare checked before opening.

### 2.8 Signage and licence display (see also section 5)

Zones: fascia board above the opening (brand and Telugu product line), a glow-sign or side projecting sign visible along the street, a window or shutter graphic, a **licence board** inside by the counter (FSSAI certificate and 14-digit number, display board, municipal licence, Legal Metrology certificate), a dated price board, a complaint-contact placard, and the temperature display. ### 2.9 POS counter, billing and CCTV placement

- Billing point at the customer end of the counter, right of the display chiller, with the scale directly in front of the cashier and the customer-facing display visible to the customer. Cash drawer below counter on the staff side.
- The same scale feeds the POS price: staff weigh, enter kg and unit in the app's POS (record-sale takes product, quantity, unit, unit price). One scale, one verified weight, one bill. Keep a spare verified scale (S/F).
- UPI QR on a stand facing the customer, sound box on counter. Bill and UPI amount must match before the cashier hands over; at day end the shift is closed with counted cash in the app (pos.mts close-shift).
- CCTV: camera 1 on the counter and cash, 2 on the cutting zone, 3 on the rear door, 4 on the street opening. Monitor placed inside, away from the customer's view. Retain footage 30 days [E]; add a "CCTV in operation" notice [U, check any privacy-notice requirement].

### 3.4 Cold-chain method and power

Temperature targets (same [U] basis as location-outlet.md; confirm with the regulation and FSSAI officer): chilled 0-4 C, poultry at 4 C or below; frozen -18 C or below; eggs in shade or chilled, away from raw meat.

| Power option | How | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engine-driven (vehicle-mounted) compressor | Belt-driven compressor on the vehicle engine or a dedicated DC unit | Real refrigeration while driving | Stops cooling when engine is off (needs electric standby); retrofit complexity; affects RTO approval | Good for option D |
| Portable generator | Petrol or diesel genset on or beside the vehicle | High power | Noise, fumes next to open meat, fuel storage, municipal and neighbour complaints | Avoid on the vehicle; backup only |
| Battery bank plus inverter (and optionally DC freezer) | 2 x 150-200 Ah deep-cycle batteries, 1.5-2 kVA inverter, charged from the alternator while driving and from mains at base overnight | Silent, clean, allows engine-off stops | Limited kWh (about 0.9-1.8 kWh usable per 150-200 Ah pair [E]); needs sizing; battery weight | **Preferred for B and C** |
| Shore (grid) power at stops | Plug into landowner's socket (with consent and safe earthing) | Cheap, unlimited | Not available everywhere; electrical safety | Use opportunistically |
| Pre-chilled mass (gel packs, eutectic plates, ice) | Frozen at base, held in insulated boxes | Zero power | Finite hold time | Always combine with the above |

Design rule [E]: size the electrical system for the display chiller plus freezer at 50% duty cycle for the whole trading day, plus 30% reserve. Roughly 0.5 kW running, 1.5-3 kWh per 6-hour shift [E]; an electrician and the body builder must confirm. Do not rely on engine idling at stops (fumes at the counter and fuel cost).

Temperature SOP: pre-chill the body and chillers at base for at least 1 hour [E]; load product at 4 C or below; log product temperature at loading, at each stop (probe in the display) and at return; discard rule [E, owner to ratify]: if the display or product is above 5 C for more than 2 hours cumulatively, or above 8 C at any point, withdraw the batch from sale and record it as wastage. Fit a data logger in the body with an external display visible to the customer.

### 3.5 Stock loading rules

- Load only what the route can sell in the shift, plus 10% [E]. Maximum carry per load: option B 80 kg, option C 200 kg [E].
- Load in the order of sale: fresh chicken in the display chiller, mutton in the middle, fish and prawn on ice in their own box, eggs in cartons above the cold area; frozen in the chest freezer. Raw above cooked is not applicable (no cooked items).
- Every pack or crate leaves base with a batch or lot number from the app's stock lots; the loader and driver sign a load sheet.
- Return rule [E]: unsold chilled product returns to base within the shift, is re-checked at 4 C or below with an intact log, and is either re-listed with a shorter use-by or written off. No stock carries over on the vehicle overnight.
- No live birds, no on-vehicle slaughter, no bone-saw. Prepare to sell packed weight where possible; weigh on a verified scale in view of the customer.

### 3.6 Hygiene and waste

- Hand-wash at open and between customers handling money; separate cashier from cutter if two staff.
- Daily wash-down at base: pressure wash, sanitise, check drains; the grey-water and waste containers are emptied at base, **never at a stop or into a road drain**.
- Waste: lidded bin inside; offal and trimmings bagged and returned to base for the unit's waste arrangement (Location agent, B8).
- Pest, dust and heat: serving hatch awning, insect-proof screens, no open product between customers.
- Staff fitness, FoSTaC supervisor and daily clean-checklist as in 2.6.

### 3.7 POS and UPI on the move

- One tablet or phone on mobile data (4G/5G; dual-SIM or hotspot as backup [E]) running the app's POS screen; a Bluetooth thermal printer is optional. Scale and tablet connected via cable or a fixed mount.
- UPI: static QR on the counter and a sound box linked to the business current account; the cashier confirms the sound-box alert before handing the product. Capture the UPI reference or last four digits in the bill note until the engineering item for UPI reference (#18) ships.
- Cash: float of a fixed amount in a locked box; counted at shift close in the app's close-shift.
- If the network drops: record sales on a paper bill book (pre-numbered) and enter them at base the same day; the app has no offline queue today (section 8, proposal P2). Never use a duplicate bill number.
- Credit sales to restaurants are not for the van; they go through the company order flow.

### 3.8 Daily route and stop planning method

**Step 1 Stop candidates.** List every candidate stop within 15 minutes of the base unit: residential clusters, markets, bus stands, offices, apartments, petrol bunks. Record landowner or authority, parking space, power, shade, footfall by hour and legality (see 3.1) in a stop register.

**Step 2 Score each stop (0-5 each, weight in brackets) [E]:** footfall in the target hour (30), non-veg buying habit and income (20), permission clarity (20), safe parking and shade (10), power access (10), distance from base (10). Reject any stop with permission score 0-1.

**Step 3 Timing.** Meat demand clusters early morning and evening [E]; avoid trading between about 11:00 and 16:00 in summer when heat spoils product [E]. Typical plan: morning shift 06:30-10:30 (2 stops), evening shift 16:30-20:30 (2 stops), 60-90 minutes per stop, 15-20 minutes drive between stops, with a mid-day return to base for restock and cleaning.

**Step 4 Weekly rotation.** Assign each stop two to three fixed weekdays so regulars learn the schedule; keep one flexible slot for markets (weekly santha days [U, ask the municipality]) and festivals. Add days around Sankranti [U], Sundays and local wedding seasons [E]; allow lower days around fasting periods such as Karthika masam [U].

**Step 5 Learn and prune.** After 4 weeks, rank stops by kg per hour and by contribution; drop the bottom 25% [E], keep a two-week trial slot for new stops.

**Step 6 Nearby towns.** One day a week [E] to Mydukur or Jammalamadugu only after the town has a confirmed stop on private land and a cold-chain check on the outward trip; carry return stock budget.

- **P5. Scale integration or confirmed-weight entry.** POS takes a typed quantity; no link to the verified scale or label printer. Propose weight-label printing and scale-linked entry for fresh weight sales.
- **P6. Vehicle and permission register.** Vehicle RC, insurance, fitness and permit expiry, plus stop permissions with landowner consent expiry, with reminders.
- **P7. Outlet kit checklist per franchise.** A pre-opening inspection record per outlet that must be signed before the first supply is allowed.
- **P8. Per-shift cost capture for mobile.** Fuel and cash expenses per shift to compute the daily economics in 3.10.
- **P9. Labels with batch and use-by from the lot.** Print customer label content (section 5) from a stock lot.
