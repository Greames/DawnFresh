# DawnFresh launch plan: Proddatur, Andhra Pradesh (target start: 10 Jan 2027)

Status legend: ⬜ not started · 🟨 in progress · ✅ done · ⛔ blocked
Owner legend: **You** = business owner · **Claude/Software** = engineering agents · **CA** = chartered accountant · **Advisor** = local consultant/lawyer

> Regulatory items below are a checklist of what normally applies to a meat business in India. Fees, thresholds and exact forms change. Confirm each with your CA, the FSSAI portal (foscos.fssai.gov.in) and Proddatur Municipality before paying or applying. Nothing here is legal advice.

## Critical path (what can delay 10 Jan)

1. Business entity and PAN/bank account → needed by almost every registration.
2. Outlet/processing premises finalised (lease signed) → needed for FSSAI, trade licence, fire/PCB, fit-out.
3. FSSAI licence + municipal meat licence → legally required before any sale. Long lead time, so start first.
4. Vendor finalisation (poultry/mutton/fish) → required before the processing dry run.
5. Software pilot readiness (issue #4) → required before the dry run.
6. Staff hired and trained → needed before the dry run (mid-Dec).

Registrations and premises are the real schedule risk, not software.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Licence delays (FSSAI, municipal) | Apply early; keep the acknowledgement; don't sell before issue |
| Premises not ready or zoning refused | Shortlist several; check municipal zoning before signing |
| Single vendor failure | Two vendors per category |
| Power cuts spoil stock | Generator/inverter, temperature log, insurance |
| Software untested against real data | Dry run on dummy data before 2 Jan |
| Franchisee model before pilot proof | Run company-owned pilot first; recruit franchisees after real margin data |
